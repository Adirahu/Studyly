Exam technique

  • Read the paper instructions carefully and answer the required number of questions.
  • For calculations, show your steps, use the correct units, and check the requested accuracy.
  • For science and geography diagrams, draw clearly and label the parts asked for.
  • For essays, plan briefly, answer the exact prompt, and leave time to review.
  • Practise past questions, then use examiner reports to learn from common mistakes.
